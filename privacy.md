# NEXUS Bot — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 23, 2026

## 1. Introduction

NEXUS Bot is a Discord application operated by Team DYNAMO. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use NEXUS Bot.

## 2. Information We Collect

NEXUS Bot may collect and process:

- **Discord User IDs:** To identify users and provide Bot features.
- **Discord Server IDs:** To identify servers and maintain server-specific settings.
- **Command and Interaction Data:** To process commands and provide requested functionality.

Our moderation features may process message information when necessary. We do not claim that message content is permanently stored unless this is separately disclosed.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Provide moderation features.
- Provide music and utility commands.
- Maintain server-specific settings.
- Identify users and servers.
- Prevent abuse and improve Bot functionality.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

The specific database and hosting provider used by NEXUS Bot have not been identified in this policy and should be confirmed before publication.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' information.

Information may be processed by Discord and relevant third-party providers when necessary to operate Bot features, including music functionality.

We may disclose information where required by applicable law.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Bot's features and meet applicable obligations.
